FAQ

Typing test questions, answered.

What is WPM?

WPM means words per minute. This app uses the common five-characters-per-word convention for speed calculations.

Does one mistake stop the test?

No. Incorrect characters are highlighted and counted, while you can continue typing and finish the session.

Does it save my results?

Results can be stored locally in your browser for personal history and statistics. No account is required for the basic experience.
