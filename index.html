FAQs

How is typing speed measured?

Typing speed is commonly expressed as words per minute, or WPM. This site calculates WPM from typed characters using the standard five-character word convention.

What is a good typing speed?

A useful target depends on your work and experience. More important than chasing a single number is improving speed while keeping accuracy high.

How is typing accuracy calculated?

Accuracy compares correctly typed characters with the characters you attempted. Mistakes reduce the final percentage.

How do I earn the certificate?

Complete a test and finish with accuracy above 98%. An eligible result automatically opens the certificate, which can be printed or saved as a PDF.
